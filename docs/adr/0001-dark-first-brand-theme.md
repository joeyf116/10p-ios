# ADR-0001 — Dark-first brand theme

## Status
Accepted

## Decision
The app uses a dark-first Material 3 theme with 10th Planet Greenville brand colors: background `#0A0A0A`, primary/accent `#CC0000`, on-primary `#FFFFFF`, surface `#1A1A1A`. A light theme is provided for accessibility but dark is the default.

## Reason
BJJ gyms have a strong dark/aggressive aesthetic. 10th Planet branding skews black and red. Dark-first avoids jarring light flashes on mobile in gym environments.
